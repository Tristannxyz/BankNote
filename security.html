Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas
1. FEEL
Substrate & Embossing
  • Security Paper: 80% cotton, 20% Philippine abaca; distinctly rough, fibrous texture (never completely smooth or slippery like bond paper).
  • Intaglio Embossed Print: Raised tactile ink across the portrait, denomination numerals, and "REPUBLIKA NG PILIPINAS".
  • Tactile Lines: Pairs of embossed short lines at edges for the visually impaired (1 to 6 pairs on paper; 5 raised dots on polymer).
2. LOOK
Watermarks & Inks
  • Shadow Watermark: Multi-tonal portrait and denomination numeral visible when held up against light.
  • Security Fibers: Red and blue fibers randomly scattered across the paper substrate front and back.
  • Asymmetric Serial Number: Digits ascending in font size from left to right.
  • See-Through Baybayin: Incomplete pre-Hispanic script that completes into "PILIPINO" against back-illumination.
3. TILT
Variable Optical Elements
  • Security Thread: Metallic windowed or embedded thread bearing "BSP" and denomination value.
  • Color-Shifting Ink (OVI): ₱1,000 numeral shifts color from green to blue when tilted.
  • Optically Variable Device (OVD): Reflective metallic patch featuring parrot (₱500) or pearl (₱1,000).
  • Polymer ₱1,000 (2022): Transparent clear window with Philippine eagle, Sampaguita, and dynamic holographic strip.

Official BSP Protocol: What To Do If You Suspect a Fake Note

Mandatory procedures under BSP Circulars and Article 168 of the Revised Penal Code of the Philippines

BSP Circular & Advisory
1
Do NOT Pass or Spend
Under Philippine law (Article 168 of the Revised Penal Code), knowingly circulating or passing counterfeit currency is punishable by imprisonment.
2
Preserve Forensic Evidence
Avoid excessive handling. Place the banknote in a clean envelope or transparent sleeve to preserve latent fingerprints and forensic marks.
3
Record Transaction Details
Note the physical appearance of the person who tendered the note, companions, date, time, and vehicle license plate number if available.
4
Surrender Immediately
Turn in the note directly to the BSP Payments & Currency Investigation Group (PCIG), nearest PNP / NBI station, or any commercial bank for a formal Confiscation Receipt.
BSP Portal: www.bsp.gov.ph
Know Your Money: bsp.gov.ph/KnowYourMoney
BSP Inquiries: (02) 8708-7087 / bspmail@bsp.gov.ph
Emergency Hotline: 911 or Local Police Station

Step 2: Best Photography Practices

Prepare Your Banknote Photo

The Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) relies on clear micro-texture patterns in intaglio engraving and abaca fibers. A high-quality photo ensures the most accurate classification.

Plain, Flat Background Lay the note completely flat on a dark, non-reflective surface.
Even Diffused Lighting Avoid harsh glare, flashlight hotspots, or casting heavy hand shadows.
All 4 Corners Visible Fill the camera frame with the banknote so all edges and borders are in view.
Sharp & In Focus Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, WEBP (up to 16MB).
