Photo Capture Tips
  • Place the note flat on a plain dark background (black or dark table).
  • Ensure even, bright lighting — avoid shadows and harsh reflections.
  • Fill the camera frame with the banknote (minimize empty background).
  • Keep camera parallel to the note — avoid angular perspective distortion.
  • Ensure the image is sharp and in focus — no blur or compression.
