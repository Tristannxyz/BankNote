How NoteGuard Works

End-to-end verification pipeline from photo capture to multi-modal verdict

1
Image Ingestion & Preprocessing
The user uploads or captures a photo of a Philippine peso note. The image is validated for integrity, normalized to standard resolution, and separated into its grayscale luminance channel for micro-texture decomposition alongside RGB planes for color spectrum analysis.
2
2D Discrete Wavelet Decomposition (DWT)
Using the Haar wavelet basis (db1), the image undergoes single-level 2D wavelet decomposition. From the approximation coefficients (cA) and detail bands (cH, cV, cD), NoteGuard extracts statistical moments that characterize genuine abaca-cotton paper fibers vs smooth photocopier paper.
3
Supervised Machine Learning Classification
The 10-dimensional feature vector is evaluated by trained Random Forest and Decision Tree classifiers. The models calculate class probabilities for Genuine vs Forged banknotes based on patterns learned from 868 Philippine currency specimens.
4
Multi-Modal Heuristics & BSP Verification
High-frequency energy (sharpness), color chemistry, and denomination patterns are checked against empirical genuine thresholds. An out-of-distribution safeguard flags unclassified images, and users are guided to verify physical FEEL • LOOK • TILT security features.

Wavelet Feature Foundations

Statistical metrics derived from wavelet approximation coefficients

Variance
Measures the spread of wavelet coefficient energy. Genuine banknotes feature micro-relief and abaca fibers that yield consistent, high variance, whereas inkjet photocopies display flat, low variance.
Skewness
Quantifies the asymmetry of the coefficient distribution. Intaglio engraved portraits produce heavy directional shading that shifts the distribution skew away from normal Gaussian noise.
Kurtosis (Excess)
Measures the peakedness and tail heaviness of wavelet coefficients. Sharp intaglio lines and fine guilloche borders produce pronounced peaks and heavy-tailed kurtosis values.
Shannon Entropy
Evaluates the information density and randomness of the banknote surface. Complex security patterns yield high entropy, while blurred or filtered counterfeit prints show reduced information entropy.

Training Data & Validation Methodology

Truthful documentation of dataset construction and machine learning validation

  • Dataset Origin: 868 high-resolution crops of Philippine banknotes across all 6 New Generation Currency (NGC) denominations (₱20, ₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱500, ₱1,000) and realistic simulated counterfeits.
  • Balanced Classes: Exactly 434 genuine specimens and 434 counterfeit specimens (50% / 50% class balance) to prevent majority-class prediction bias.
  • Data Leakage Prevention: Split 80% train (685 images) and 20% test (183 images) using GroupShuffleSplit grouped by source image, guaranteeing no augmented versions of the same banknote appear in both training and test sets.
  • Domain Adaptation: Feature extraction includes adaptive resolution scaling and empirical bounding to protect against out-of-distribution camera anomalies.

Academic & Educational Project Disclaimer

NoteGuard is an academic machine learning proof-of-concept developed to demonstrate automated wavelet texture analysis. It is NOT an officially certified currency verification device and should never be used as the sole determinant for legal or commercial transactions. Always physically verify banknotes using the official FEEL • LOOK • TILT guidelines published by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP).
