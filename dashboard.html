How to Use NoteGuard

Philippine Peso Banknote Verification Guide
Step 1 of 6
Step 1: Getting Started

Open the Verify Page

Navigate to the Verify page using the top navigation bar. This is NoteGuard's primary interactive testing suite where you can inspect Philippine Peso banknotes for counterfeit characteristics.

NoteGuard Verify Security Features Dashboard
You can access NoteGuard from any modern desktop, tablet, or smartphone browser.
Step 2: Best Photography Practices

Prepare Your Banknote Photo

The Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) relies on clear micro-texture patterns in intaglio engraving and abaca fibers. A high-quality photo ensures the most accurate classification.

Plain, Flat Background Lay the note completely flat on a dark, non-reflective surface.
Even Diffused Lighting Avoid harsh glare, flashlight hotspots, or casting heavy hand shadows.
All 4 Corners Visible Fill the camera frame with the banknote so all edges and borders are in view.
Sharp & In Focus Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, WEBP (up to 16MB).
Step 3: Uploading

Upload the Banknote Image

Drag and drop your image directly onto the dashed upload zone, or click "Choose file" to browse from your device storage or camera roll.

Drag & drop banknote photo or click Choose file
Quick Demo Specimens Available: ₱20 ₱50 ₱100 ₱200 ₱500 ₱1,000
Step 4: Inspection

Run Machine Learning Analysis

Click the golden "Analyze Currency Note" button. NoteGuard instantly executes full wavelet decomposition and color gamut extraction:

1
DWT Decomposition: Extracts 4 Daubechies (db1) sub-bands (Approximation cA, Horizontal cH, Vertical cV, Diagonal cD).
2
Gamut Moments: Measures 6 RGB color distribution metrics to verify authentic government security dyes.
3
Ensemble Classification: Evaluated via trained Random Forest and Decision Tree models.
Step 5: Evaluation

Understand Verification Results

Review the resulting verdict card, model confidence score, and extracted wavelet values:

GENUINE
High texture relief and color moments consistent with authentic BSP currency specimens.
FORGED
Texture variance, intaglio sharpness, or security ink deviations detected.
Yellow Out-of-Distribution Warning: If features fall far outside normal banknote ranges, lighting was uneven or non-currency was scanned. Retake the photo following the tips in Step 2.
Step 6: Advanced Tools

Explore More NoteGuard Features

Expand your understanding of banknote analysis using NoteGuard's specialized analytical tools:

Developer Testing: Test custom numerical feature vectors or specimen vectors from the training set.
Evaluation Dashboard: Review accuracy, precision, recall, F1-scores, confusion matrices, and ROC curves.
Security Features Guide: Interactive FEEL • LOOK • TILT checklist for all NGC paper and polymer notes.
